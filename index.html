WHY THIS DATA MATTERS

The invisible
becomes inspectable.

Safecast is a volunteer-driven nonprofit that has collected and openly published environmental measurements since 2011.

Its dataset is unusual because it combines geographic coordinates, timestamps, units, and observations from a distributed citizen-science effort. That makes it possible to build independent maps and research tools from the source records rather than relying only on a pre-rendered visualization.

WHY IT'S SPECIAL

Many public APIs focus on popular commercial categories. Safecast exposes a substantial, open environmental dataset collected outside a single centralized sensor network.
